# Connect for Good

In the existing system, volunteer recruitment for social impact projects typically relies on manual processes, such as word-of-mouth, physical sign-up sheets, or limited online platforms.These methods often lack efficiency, scalability, and effective matching of volunteers to projects. It can be challenging for organizations to find volunteers with the desired skills, while volunteers may struggle to find projects that align with their interests and expertise.

Connect For Good is a platform crafted to link volunteers with social impact projects, streamlining the matchmaking process. Organizations can post their projects and detail the necessary skills and expertise. Meanwhile, developers and volunteers can showcase their skills and interests through profiles, enabling the platform to match them with fitting projects. This system fosters meaningful collaborations and boosts the efficiency of volunteer recruitment for social impact endeavors.
